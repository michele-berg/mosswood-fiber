About the instructor

For more than 30 years, I have been immersed in the world of fiber arts, with hands-on experience in knitting, crocheting, weaving, spinning, silkworm rearing, and silk reeling.

Teaching has been an important part of my journey: I have taught fiber arts classes across the Society for Creative Anachronism for over 20 years, and have taught several years at the Dallas-Fort Worth Fiber Fest.

My teaching style focuses on clear, supportive instruction that builds confidence and helps students understand the why behind the techniques they learn. I am passionate about preserving historic textile traditions while also encouraging modern creative exploration.

I live in Austin with my husband and our beloved collection of cats, dogs, horses, and chickens, where our home studio is always full of yarn, fiber, and ongoing experiments.
