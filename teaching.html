Workshops and fiber gatherings

Knitting classes with patient, practical instruction

Classes are built around clear demonstrations, hands-on practice, and an emphasis on understanding the structure behind the technique. Confirmed dates and locations can be added here.

3 hrBeginner

Beginner Knitting: Core Skills for New Stitchers

Start with long-tail and knit cast-ons, knit and purl stitches, binding off, tension control, reading your work, fixing common mistakes, joining new yarn, and beginner stitch patterns including garter, stockinette, ribbing, and seed stitch.

Skill level: true beginner · Homework: none · Students leave with a swatch sampler.

3 hrNext Step

Advanced Beginner Knitting: Building Skills & Confidence

For students who can already cast on, knit, purl, bind off, and work basic stitch patterns. Covers written patterns, basic charts, gauge, swatches, shaping, knitting in the round, and an overview of circular needles, DPNs, and magic loop.

Homework: 6 inch by 6 inch stockinette swatch, knit flat.

2 hrCables

Knitting Cables: Twist Your Knitting

Learn front-cross and back-cross cable anatomy, how to use a cable needle, how to read cable charts and written instructions, and how to troubleshoot common cable mistakes.

Skill level: comfortable with cast on, bind off, knit, purl, and pattern reading.

2 hrColor

Colorwork Confidence: Your First Stranded Knitting Project

Practice reading colorwork charts, managing and catching floats, maintaining even tension, understanding color dominance, and troubleshooting common stranded-knitting mistakes.

Students create a small colorwork swatch using two highly contrasting solid colors.

About the instructor

The instructor behind Mosswood Fiber Studio has been immersed in fiber arts for more than 30 years, with experience across knitting, crocheting, weaving, spinning, silkworm rearing, and silk reeling. She has taught fiber arts throughout the Society for Creative Anachronism for over 20 years and has taught for several years at the Dallas-Fort Worth Fiber Fest. Her teaching style is clear, supportive, and focused on helping students understand both the how and the why.
